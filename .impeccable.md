# Design Context — The Fastnet

**Target audience**: Locals and tourists in Barceloneta (Barcelona's beachside/harbour district) looking for an authentic Irish bar & restaurant experience — casual drop-in drinkers, groups watching sport, diners wanting pub food, and visitors drawn in by the harbourside location at Pg. de Joan de Borbó 22.

**Use cases**: Browse the menu (tapas, mains, sandwiches, burgers) before or during a visit; check opening hours/location to walk in; see real Google reviews and photos to decide whether to go; reserve a table; find the place on the map from the harbour promenade.

**Brand personality/tone**: Warm, rustic, unpretentious Irish pub — proper Guinness, dark wood, brass fittings, harbour-town character. Named after the Fastnet Rock lighthouse off the Irish coast, so there's a subtle nautical thread (the harbour setting reinforces this) without tipping into kitsch "nautical theme park." Should feel lived-in and genuine, not polished/corporate — a real pub, not a chain.

**Established visual system** (already locked in, not open for re-litigation during this audit):
- Palette: dark wood/forest-green backgrounds (`--wood-dark-a`, `--forest-dk-a`) with cream/brass accents — no light mode, this is an intentionally dark, warm, pub-interior aesthetic.
- Display font: Cinzel Decorative (700 only, self-hosted) — an ornate Roman-inspired display face used for headings/logo type; body/label font is Lato.
- The decorative font has genuine tight glyph collisions by design at large sizes (confirmed via glyph-metrics analysis, not a bug). One deliberate exception: the hero's "GOOD" O-O overlap is an intentional decorative flourish the client wants kept. The footer's "THE FASTNET" collisions were separately fixed with targeted per-letter kerning spans.
- Reviews are real Google review data (not placeholder), styled as dark cards to match the rest of the site.
- Logo: hand-lettered script wordmark with a harp icon either side, cream-on-transparent, used on dark backgrounds only.

**Constraints**: Static HTML/CSS/JS site (no framework), trilingual EN/ES/CA via `data-i18n`, deploys to Vercel from repo root, WCAG AA target, mobile-first with a 90+ Lighthouse mobile target.
