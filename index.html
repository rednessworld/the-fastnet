Our Story

A Proper Irish Pub, Barcelona Side

The Fastnet brings the real thing to Passeig de Joan de Borbó — a well-kept pint of Guinness, Murphy's on tap, and a whiskey shelf worth taking your time over. Rugby and football play on the screens, and the regulars mix easily with whoever's just walked in off the port.

The kitchen keeps it honest: tapas boards, hearty curries, stacked burgers and plates built to go with a pint, not compete with it.
